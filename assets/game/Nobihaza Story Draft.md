# ACT - 1 coast ig?



## scene 1

Scene 1: Beach House Spawn



CUTSCENE (fade-in):



Camera fades from black.



Nobita is lying on the floor of a beach bungalow (the kind they stayed at during summer holidays).



His glasses are missing, vision blurred, colors washed out.



Outside, faint waves crash against the shore.



Nobita (waking up, dazed):

“Eh...? W-Where am I??? D-Doraemon…?”

(He slowly sits up, looking around nervously.)

“This isn’t right… Weren’t we just here for our summer vacation? O-Oh no… I better look around…!”



GAMEPLAY STARTS:



Screen blur effect until glasses are found.



Player can move around the bungalow, interact with objects.



Puzzle 1: Glasses



Objective: Find Nobita’s glasses (on a shelf, under a pillow, or on the floor).



When picked up → screen sharpens.



Nobita (relieved):

“…Ah, that’s better. Now, what’s this…?”



Puzzle 2: Notes



Papers scattered on the desk / floor. When examined:



Mentions of “Virus… T, H… unstable mutations”.



Signed in handwriting that resembles Doraemon’s.



Nobita (reading aloud):

“V-Virus...? T...? H-Hey, this handwriting… it looks just like Doraemon’s!”



Puzzle 3: Doraemon’s Door



Player finds Doraemon’s Anywhere Door in the corner.



Interacts → door does nothing.



Nobita (panicking):

“N-no way… it’s totally dead! Doraemoooon, help!!”



Puzzle: Activate a fuse box / generator nearby to power it.



After power restores, the door glows faintly but won’t fully activate.



CUTSCENE (resolve moment):

Nobita (calling out): “Doraemon… Shizuka… anyone…?!”

(Silence. He trembles, then clenches his fists.)

Nobita: “…No. I can’t wait for anyone else. If I want to go home—”

Nobita (shouting): “I need to find a way to do this myself!!!!!”



GAMEPLAY RESUMES:



Player leaves the bungalow.



Camera pans slowly across the empty, abandoned coastline: umbrellas collapsed, toys in the sand, but no people.



Nobita (nervously):

“Not a single person…? This feels… wrong.”



→ End of Scene 1.



## Scene 2

Exploration Gameplay: Interactable Objects



Beach Umbrella \& Towels



Towels still damp as if someone just left.



Nobita: “…It’s like they disappeared in the middle of swimming…”



Sandcastle with Toy Shovel



Half-built, water washing it away.



When inspected, Nobita hears faint giggling → but no one is there.



Nobita (jumps): “Eeeh?! I-I could’ve sworn… I heard something!”



Cooler Box



Inside: melted ice, half-eaten popsicles, swarmed by ants.



Nobita: “Y-You mean… someone was just eating here? Then where did they go…?”



Beach Ball (Physics Object)



Rolls toward Nobita when the wind gusts.



When kicked → it pops with a loud hiss.



Nobita (yelps): “Waaah! D-don’t scare me like that!”



Abandoned Camera Tripod



Camera still recording → playback shows a family waving happily on the same beach yesterday.



Last second of video: static, screaming in the background.



Nobita: “…This was filmed… yesterday…?!”



Footprints in the Sand



Leading toward jungle → look human at first, but warped/clawed at the end.



Nobita: “…Th-these don’t look normal… at all…”



Suitcase by the Shore



Locked → Nobita can break it open.



Inside: scattered notes about evacuation orders + virus breach, plus a bloodied handkerchief.



Nobita: “E-evacuate…? Viral… breach? Wh-what does this mean?!”



Broken Lifeguard Stand



Binoculars left behind. Player can zoom in and spot:



A distant figure standing in the water … but when Nobita lowers binoculars, it’s gone.



Nobita (panicking): “I-I-I definitely saw someone!! Didn’t I?!”



Atmosphere Cues While Exploring



Random gust of wind knocks a beach chair over (jump scare).



Distant muffled groan from a locked beach shack.



Seagull lands, pecks at the sand → reveals a bloodstained cloth.



Ending Beat (Transition)



As Nobita follows the warped footprints inland, music fades to low drones.



He stares into the dark jungle.

Nobita (gulping):

“…If Doraemon’s here… he must’ve gone this way. A-Alright, Nobita… just… be brave…”



→End of scene 2
